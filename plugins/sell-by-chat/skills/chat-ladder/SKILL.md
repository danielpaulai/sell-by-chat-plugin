# The Chat Ladder

The whole Sell by Chat method in one drawing. Studio id: `chatladder` (The Studio, Model Lab). Six DM bubbles climb one dashed line: a conversation that moves one rung at a time, never two. The red lands on the ask.

## When to draw it

- The founder is teaching or pitching the method and wants it on one page.
- A conversation stalled and they cannot see which rung it is on.
- They pitched too early: point at the ladder, show how many rungs they skipped.

## Anatomy (never deviate)

- **Q1 EASY**: one easy question, broad, zero pressure
- **Q2 DEEPER**: the hardest part of what they said
- **Q3 COMMIT**: do they want it fixed now
- **GIVE**: free help, no strings, before any ask
- **THE ASK**: one call, two concrete times (the only red)
- **IF QUIET**: follow up day 1, 3, 7
- Punch: **Serve first. Ask small.**
- Footnote: every message runs lube, value, offer.

## Step labels contract

Editable Studio fields: `easy`, `deep`, `commit`, `give`, `ask`, `follow`, `punch`. Every label 5 words or fewer, in the founder's words where possible. Never use an em dash.

## Red-last rule

Structure first, labels second, one red accent last. Red `#F01621` belongs to THE ASK bubble and its two time chips only. Nothing else on the page is red.

## Deliver

1. Confirm the six labels with the founder, one at a time if they want to customize.
2. Insert Studio model `chatladder`, or draw along rung by rung: bubble, kicker, label, next rung.
3. Close on the punch: serve first, ask small.
