# First Week: from 5 to 25 without the fear

Nobody goes from zero to 25 on day one. The ramp: day 1 and 2, five starts. Day 3 and 4, ten. Day 5, fifteen. Week two, twenty five. Tell them this out loud so the small numbers feel like the plan, not like failing.

## Each day

1. Ask what day of the ramp they are on (or read the scoreboard and count).
2. Give them today's number and today's focus, one sentence each:
   - Day 1: five openers to the warmest people. Focus: press send. Nothing else matters today.
   - Day 2: five more. Focus: reply to anyone who wrote back, using LVO.
   - Day 3: ten. Focus: one full three-questions chat with whoever is warmest.
   - Day 4: ten. Focus: send your give to at least one person.
   - Day 5: fifteen. Focus: your first ask, two real times, framed as help.
   - Weekend: rest, or light replies only. The wheel keeps credit for the week.
3. Draft their messages the normal way (see /daily-chats), then remind them to log the scoreboard.

## What to expect (say this before they worry)

- Most people do not reply. That is normal, about 3 in 10 do, and slowly.
- The first reply usually comes day 1 or 2. Celebrate it loudly.
- The first booked call usually comes in week one or two. When it happens, tell them to save what worked (see the wins bank in /scoreboard) and to tell their squad.
- If 50 sends get zero replies, the opener needs new words, not more volume. Send them to /score-my-dm with their opener.

## Words

Very simple words. Warm. This founder is nervous, and the job of this skill is to make day one feel small and day seven feel inevitable.
