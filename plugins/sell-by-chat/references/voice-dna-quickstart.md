# Voice DNA quickstart · when the founder has no VOICE-DNA.md

Never block setup because the file is missing. Build a starter Voice DNA in 10 minutes, right here.

## Option 1 · They have the Purely Personal brain

If their business brain or Purely Personal files are reachable (a connected server, or a folder they can point to), pull voice and ICP from there and write VOICE-DNA.md into this folder. Confirm what you found in three lines and move on.

## Option 2 · Build it from their real writing (best)

Ask them to paste 3 to 5 things they actually wrote: LinkedIn posts, emails to clients, WhatsApp messages. Real writing only, not marketing copy someone polished. From it, write a one-page VOICE-DNA.md with:

- **Sound**: formal or casual, short or long sentences, emoji or not
- **Their words**: 5 to 10 phrases they actually use
- **Their fillers and openers**: how they start messages, how they greet
- **Never**: words and shapes that are not them

Show it to them, fix what they push back on, save it.

## Option 3 · The 5-question interview (no writing available)

1. "How do you greet someone you like but have not spoken to in a while? Type it exactly."
2. "Your last client win, tell me about it in two sentences, exactly how you would voice-note a friend."
3. "Formal or casual? Emoji or not?"
4. "Three words or phrases you say all the time."
5. "One thing you would NEVER say."

Write the one-page VOICE-DNA.md from the answers. It will be rough. Tell them: every draft they edit makes it sharper, and after a week of chats it will sound like them.

## Same for the ICP

If there is no ICP file, two questions: "Who do you help, said the way THEY would describe themselves?" and "What is the problem, in the exact words they say out loud?" Save as ICP.md. Done. Never ask more than this during setup, they can deepen it later.
