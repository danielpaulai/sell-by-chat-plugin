# Platform playbook · same method, different doorbell

The five moves and the Chat Path never change. What changes per platform: what a raised hand looks like, how the chat sounds, and how the give and the ask travel. Read the founder's platform from SELL-BY-CHAT.md and write in that platform's register. A founder can run more than one platform; each contact lives on ONE platform in the pipeline.

## LinkedIn

- **Raised hands:** comments on your posts, likes from your ICP, profile views, new connection requests, replies to a hand-raiser.
- **Where the chat lives:** DMs.
- **Sound:** professional but human. Full sentences, warm, zero corporate speak. At most one emoji.
- **Opener norms:** thank the specific comment or name the specific post. Profile views get "saw you stopped by, good to connect properly."
- **The give:** a PDF, a checklist, a doc link. Sent in the DM, no sign-up page.
- **The ask:** 15 minutes, two real times, or a calendar link plus the confirm move.
- **Quirks:** connection request notes are limited, keep them under 200 characters or send none and message after accepting. Weekday mornings get the best reply rates.

## Instagram

- **Raised hands:** story replies (the warmest signal there is), poll and question-box taps, comments, new followers who fit the ICP, DMs about a post.
- **Where the chat lives:** DMs, often starting from a story reply thread.
- **Sound:** casual, short, emoji welcome, voice notes are normal once warm. Sentence fragments are fine.
- **Opener norms:** react to what THEY replied to. "Ha, glad that one landed 😂 how did the launch go?" Never formal.
- **The give:** a link in DM, or "want me to send it here?" A voice note explaining one tip counts as a give and feels very personal.
- **The ask:** keep it light: "want to jump on a quick call about it? I have Tue 11 or Wed 2."
- **Quirks:** links can look spammy to new contacts, offer before sending ("want the link?"). Story replies land in Requests for non-followers, check that folder daily.

## WhatsApp

- **Raised hands:** replies to your status, someone saving your number, a referral intro, a group member reacting to something you shared, past clients and warm contacts going quiet.
- **Where the chat lives:** it IS the chat. WhatsApp is for people who already know you, never cold strangers. The warmest channel, treat it with the most care.
- **Sound:** exactly like texting a friend. Short bursts, two or three messages in a row is normal, emoji and voice notes are native. The name-only opener ("Sarah!") works best here.
- **Opener norms:** reconnect warmly: "you crossed my mind today, how did the rebrand land?" Status replies: answer the human first, business later.
- **The give:** send the file straight in the chat, or a 60-second voice note with one tip. Voice notes build more trust per second than anything else in this playbook.
- **The ask:** often you can skip the calendar: "are you free for a call Thursday afternoon?" and agree a time right in the chat. For small offers this is THE platform for the in-chat close: the plain note, "just reply I'M IN."
- **Quirks:** never add anyone to a group or broadcast list without asking. Business hours matter more here, it is a personal space. If they have your number from a lead magnet or event, remind them who you are in one line first.

## Facebook

- **Raised hands:** comments in groups you run or belong to, reactions on your posts, replies to a hand-raiser in a group, friend requests from your niche.
- **Where the chat lives:** Messenger.
- **Sound:** neighborly. Warmer than LinkedIn, calmer than Instagram.
- **Opener norms:** reference the group or the thread: "loved your comment in [group] about X."
- **The give:** a link or file in Messenger, or a group resource post they get tagged in.
- **The ask:** same as LinkedIn: 15 minutes, two times, confirm move.
- **Quirks:** messages from non-friends land in Message Requests and are easy to miss, say so when you switch someone from a group thread to DM. Respect group rules, never pitch inside someone else's group.

## TikTok

- **Raised hands:** comments, follows from ICP accounts, video replies, shares.
- **Where the chat lives:** comment replies first, then DMs once they follow.
- **Sound:** fast, playful, very short. This is the most casual register of all.
- **Opener norms:** reply to their comment publicly with warmth, then "sent you a DM" once there is a real thread.
- **The give:** a link in DM, or point them to a specific video of yours that answers their exact question.
- **The ask:** move it off-platform early: "easier to talk properly on a call or WhatsApp, want to?"
- **Quirks:** DMs may be restricted between non-mutuals, the comment thread does more of the early work here than on any other platform.

## Pacing, on every platform

25 starts a day is the ceiling, everywhere. 30 to 90 seconds between sends, varied, never a fixed rhythm. A break every 8 to 10 messages. New accounts: half of everything. Fast and regular looks like a machine, and machines get restricted.

## Moving someone between platforms

The chat follows the person, not the app. When a conversation gets real on a public platform, offering the warmer channel is itself a trust move: "this is easier on WhatsApp if you want, here is my number." Record the move in the pipeline (their entry keeps one home platform, note the switch). Never move someone colder than they are: TikTok to DM is fine, LinkedIn to WhatsApp needs a reason.
