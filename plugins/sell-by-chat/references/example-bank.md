# Example bank · imitate the shape, never copy the words

Real-shaped conversations to learn from. Match the founder's own voice and facts, keep these shapes.

## Openers that get replies

**After a comment (LinkedIn):**
> Hey Sarah, thanks for the comment on the VA post. Appreciate you. What finally pushed you to hire one?

Why it works: thank you first, names the exact post, one easy question, zero pitch.

**After a profile view (LinkedIn):**
> Hey Marcus, saw you stopped by my profile. Good to connect properly. What are you building these days?

Why it works: no "why did you view my profile" weirdness. Warm, light, easy to answer.

**After a story reply (Instagram):**
> Ha, glad that one landed 😂 how's the launch going, last I saw you were two weeks out?

Why it works: matches the platform's energy, remembers something about them.

**Reconnecting with someone who went quiet months ago:**
> Hey Priya, you crossed my mind today. How did the rebrand land in the end?

Why it works: humans reconnect like this. No apology for the silence, no pitch.

## A full chat, opener to booked call

> **You:** Hey Jon, thanks for the comment on my post today. Appreciate you.
> **Jon:** Of course! That one hit home, honestly.
> **You:** Ha, you and half my feed. What is eating most of your week right now?  *(easy question)*
> **Jon:** Content, if I'm honest. I write everything myself at night.
> **You:** Been there, the midnight shift is brutal. What have you tried so far to get it off your plate?  *(lube, then deeper)*
> **Jon:** Tried a VA, tried batching. Nothing stuck.
> **You:** Makes sense, both break for the same reason. I made a 1-page checklist on exactly this, want it? No sign-up, just the PDF.  *(lube, then value)*
> **Jon:** Yes please, that would be great.
> **You:** Sent! And straight question: is this something you want fixed this quarter, or is it a next-year thing?  *(commit question)*
> **Jon:** This quarter. It's costing me clients.
> **You:** Then it sounds fixable. Want to take 15 minutes and look at it together? If it's a simple fix, I'll tell you. If not, I'll show you your options. I have Tuesday 11am or Wednesday 2pm.  *(the ask, framed as help)*
> **Jon:** Tuesday works.

Count the beats: five messages from the founder before any ask. One question at a time. The give came before the offer. The ask used Jon's own problem.

## Follow-ups that do not feel like chasing

**Day 1 (they went quiet after a good exchange):**
> No rush on my last message. Meanwhile, this reminded me of what you said about the launch: [one useful link or thought]

**Day 3:**
> Sent you that checklist, curious if the audit step surprised you. It usually does.

**Day 7:**
> Saw [something in their world: their post, their industry news] and thought of you. Hope the week is treating you well.

Notice: every touch GIVES. None of them say "just following up".

## What bad looks like (never produce these)

> "Hi! I help ambitious founders leverage AI to optimize their growth. Would love to hop on a quick call!"
Why it fails: about the sender, jargon, pitch in message one, "quick call" to a stranger.

> "Hey, did you see my last message?"
Why it fails: guilt with zero value. Deletes trust.
