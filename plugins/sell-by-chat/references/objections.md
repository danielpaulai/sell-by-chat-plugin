# Brush-offs · what to say when they push back

Every answer runs LVO: feel seen, give, open one door. Never defend, never chase, never discount in the DMs.

## "How much is it?"

> Fair question. It depends on what actually needs fixing, and I'd be guessing right now. That's exactly what the 15 minutes is for: if it's a simple fix I'll tell you and it costs nothing. Want Tuesday 11am or Wednesday 2pm?

Never quote a price in the chat. Price without diagnosis reads as a menu, not a doctor.

## "Can you just send me some info?"

> Happy to. The most useful thing I have is [the give], sending it now. Most people read it and know within five minutes if this is their problem. If it is, we'll talk. Fair?

Give real value, no strings. "Info" requests are usually a polite exit; the give finds out which ones are real.

## "Not right now / too busy"

> Totally get it, and honestly that's the answer most people give right before the busy gets worse. No pressure from me. Can I check back in a month, or would you rather I leave it with you?

Respect the no. Ask permission for the future touch. Then actually come back in a month with value, not a pitch.

## "Who are you? / Is this a sales thing?"

> Ha, fair. I'm [name], I [niche sentence in plain words]. You commented on my post so I said hi, that's the whole plot. What are you working on?

Disarm with honesty and lightness. Never get defensive.

## They ghost after the ask

Do not repeat the ask. Run the day 1 / 3 / 7 rhythm with value only. Most yeses arrive after the fifth touch, and they arrive because you kept giving, not because you kept asking.
