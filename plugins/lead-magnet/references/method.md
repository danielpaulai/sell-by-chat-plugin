# The Tiny Win Method (shared reference for every skill)

A lead magnet is a **tiny win**: the warm bite at a bakery sample counter. One small, instant, useful thing that proves the whole bakery. A lead is anyone you have permission to contact (email, follower, open DM).

## The Magnet Map
Score any magnet idea: value (hot/cold topic) x friction (threat + hassle).
- TRAP: cold + threat ("free strategy call")
- DUST: cold + easy ("join my newsletter") - the newsletter is the container, never the bait
- VAULT: hot + heavy (the ultimate guide, the challenge) - juice not worth the squeeze
- SNAP: hot + effortless - the instant yes. Only build here.

## The five moves
1. **Pick it** - one splinter (small, irritating, right-now problem, in the client's spoken words) for one named person ("that's me" specific). 3 problems x 3 splinters, circle one.
2. **Pack it** - the machine, not the manual. Done-for-you format (prompt, template, script, scorecard, checklist, calculator), 5 steps max, covers step ONE of the bigger method. Test: "could I have posted this and lost nothing?" The fix already exists in an owned asset - shrink, never create from scratch.
3. **Name it** - must "sound like a thing" (The ___). Headline recipe: small number + the thing + concrete outcome + timeframe + (without the pain). Small promises are believable. Swap test: a competitor could not run it unchanged.
4. **Post it** - the hand-raiser, platform-neutral: "I just made [THING] for [PERSON] who wants [RESULT]. Want a copy? Drop [KEYWORD] in the comments." No funnel needed. Every comment is a lead. Delivery is BY EMAIL so the opt-in IS the newsletter subscription.
5. **Point it** - magnet anatomy is Problem / Fix / Pointer. The pointer sells the NEXT step (waitlist with real date+seats, reply, call, training, community, second keyword), never the end step, never another snack. The snack sells the meal.

## The follow-through
- **Seven-Day Echo**: Day 0 deliver, Day 1 quick win (zero selling), Day 3 belief shift (offer once, in passing), Day 5 client story (proof not pitch), Day 7 the plain ask. Ratio: useful, useful, useful, proof, ask.
- **Counter Scripts**: 4 DM replies written BEFORE posting - delivery ("where do I send it?" + one small question), thanks, real question, day-3 silence (give something new, no guilt). Pitch only when they ask what you do.
- **The 24-Hour Post**: score (Tiny 2X, Named 2X, Instant, Posted, Pointed; 12/14 = ship), then post inside 24 hours. Posted beats perfect.

## Voice rules (all output)
Plain English, short sentences, warm, second person, important things said twice, NO em dashes, no hype words, no invented numbers or clients (mark unknowns [SUGGESTED] or ask). Platform-neutral: never assume LinkedIn.
