# Calculator magnet

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/method.md`. The strongest claim a magnet can make: "see YOUR number in 60 seconds."

Build spec:
1. One number out, 3 to 5 inputs in - all inputs things the buyer knows by heart (hours, price, clients, no-shows). More inputs = homework.
2. The output screen does three jobs: the number big, one line of what it means ("you are losing roughly [X] per month to [SPLINTER]"), and one line of what to do (the pointer).
3. The math must be defensible: show the formula in small print. Conservative assumptions, stated. Never a scare-number you cannot defend in the DMs.
4. Ship it via /lead-magnet:build-the-file as a working single-file HTML tool (vanilla JS, branded, mobile-first) with a printable summary view.
5. Include 3 result bands (like a scorecard) so every number lands in a named situation with a next step.

Anti-patterns: 10-input spreadsheets, magic numbers with hidden math, outputs that only say "it depends".
