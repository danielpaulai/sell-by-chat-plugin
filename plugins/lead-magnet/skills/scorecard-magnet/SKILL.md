# Scorecard magnet

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/method.md`.

Build spec:
1. 7 to 10 yes/no or 0-2 questions, each one observable behavior ("did more than ten people..."), never feelings. The questions themselves must teach - reading the list shows them the gap.
2. Three honest bands with names and consequences (green: what to protect; yellow: the one fix; red: what it is costing them). Never a quiz that always says "you need help" - a real green must be reachable.
3. One line per band on the first action. The red and yellow bands' action IS the pointer.
4. Format for self-scoring on paper or phone: tick, count, read your band. Under 5 minutes.
5. Offer the AI upgrade: a short prompt they can paste with their answers to get a personalized read.

Anti-patterns: vague questions, flattery bands, more than 10 items, hiding the scoring logic.
