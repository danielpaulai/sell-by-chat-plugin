# The Seven-Day Echo

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/method.md` AND `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/email-templates.md` (the copy-paste skeletons - start from them, never from scratch). Write five finished emails (subject + preview + body, 80-180 words, email 5 up to 250):

- Day 0 DELIVER: link early, where to start, one line on what's coming, a reply prompt. No selling.
- Day 1 QUICK WIN: one move from inside the magnet. Zero selling, not even a P.S.
- Day 3 BELIEF SHIFT: teach the reframe behind the splinter; the offer appears in exactly one passing sentence, no link pressure.
- Day 5 STORY: a real client story with a real number (ask for it, never invent), the common objection resolved inside the story.
- Day 7 THE ASK: plain and unembarrassed - what it is, price, who it is for, who it is not for, one action. Urgency only if real; if none, write without it and say what they could truthfully create.

Ratio is sacred: useful, useful, useful, proof, ask. Phone-sized paragraphs, "you" constantly, one idea per email, all five openings different, end each with something to do. Finish with: the arc read as one story, the sentence most likely to get a reply, and the note that whoever replies to email 2 with a real question is the warmest lead of the week.
